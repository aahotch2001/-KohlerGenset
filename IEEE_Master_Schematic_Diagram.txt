================================================================================
  KOHLER 60kW GENSET — IEEE SCHEMATIC CIRCUIT DIAGRAM & COMPONENT SYMBOLS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SCHEMATIC COMPONENT SYMBOL LEGEND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  /\/\/\       = Resistor (R)
  ---||---     = Fixed Ceramic Capacitor (C)
  ---|\|<|---  = Diode / LED (D)
  ---[ / ]---  = Mechanical Relay Switch / Contact (COM / NO)
  ---( m )---  = Relay Inductor Coil / SSR Input
  ---( V )---  = DC Voltage Source / Battery
  ---_|_-----  = Common Ground (GND Bus)
  ---[===]---  = Inline Safety Fuse (F)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. POWER SUPPLY & VOLTAGE REGULATION SCHEMATIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 12V Generator Battery (+) ----[=== 5A Fuse]----+----> DROK Buck Converter [IN+]
                                                |
 12V Generator Battery (-) ---------------------+----> DROK Buck Converter [IN-]
                                                |
                                               _|_ (GND)

 DROK Buck Converter Output:
   [OUT+] --------------------------------------------> +5.0V Soldered Power Bus Rail
   [OUT-] --------------------------------------------> Common Ground Bus Rail (_|_)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DUAL ADS1115 16-BIT ADC MODULE SCHEMATICS & ANALOG TAP DIVIDERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. ADS1115 Module #1 (Address 0x48 - ADDR connected to GND):

   [VDD]  <------------------------------------------- +5.0V Power Bus Rail
   [GND]  <------------------------------------------- Common Ground Bus Rail (_|_)
   [ADDR] <------------------------------------------- Common Ground Bus Rail (_|_)
   [SDA]  <------------------------------------------- ELEGOO Terminal [D21] (Green Wire)
   [SCL]  <------------------------------------------- ELEGOO Terminal [D22] (White Wire)

   Channel A0 (Oil Pressure Gauge Sender Terminal 'S'):
     Kohler Oil Sender [S] ---/\/\/\---(10k Ω)---+---> ADS1115 Pin [A0] (Blue Wire)
                                                 |
                                              /\/\/\ (22k Ω Pull-Down)
                                                 |
                                              ---||--- (0.1µF / 104 Capacitor)
                                                 |
                                                _|_ (Common GND Bus)

   Channel A1 (Water Temperature Gauge Sender Terminal 'S'):
     Kohler Temp Sender [S] ---/\/\/\---(10k Ω)---+---> ADS1115 Pin [A1] (Purple Wire)
                                                 |
                                              /\/\/\ (22k Ω Pull-Down)
                                                 |
                                              ---||--- (0.1µF / 104 Capacitor)
                                                 |
                                                _|_ (Common GND Bus)

   Channel A2 (12V Starter Battery Voltage Sense):
     12V Battery (+) ----------/\/\/\---(10k Ω)---+---> ADS1115 Pin [A2] (Green/Yellow Wire)
                                                 |      [Calibrated Multiplier: 7.450]
                                              /\/\/\ (2.2k Ω Pull-Down)
                                                 |
                                                _|_ (Common GND Bus)

   Channel A3 (48V Solar Battery Bank Voltage Sense):
     48V Solar Bank (+) -------/\/\/\---(47k Ω)---+---> ADS1115 Pin [A3] (Black/Orange Wire)
                                                 |      [Calibrated Multiplier: 18.407]
                                              /\/\/\ (2.7k Ω Pull-Down)
                                                 |
                                                _|_ (Common GND Bus)


B. ADS1115 Module #2 (Address 0x49 - ADDR connected to 3V3):

   [VDD]  <------------------------------------------- +5.0V Power Bus Rail
   [GND]  <------------------------------------------- Common Ground Bus Rail (_|_)
   [ADDR] <------------------------------------------- ELEGOO Terminal [3V3] (Sets Address 0x49)
   [SDA]  <------------------------------------------- ELEGOO Terminal [D21] (Green Wire - Daisy Chained)
   [SCL]  <------------------------------------------- ELEGOO Terminal [D22] (White Wire - Daisy Chained)

   Channel A0 (5 PSI Hydrostatic Fuel Level Transducer):
     5 PSI Fuel Transducer Yellow Wire --------------> ADS1115 #2 Pin [A0] (0.5V-4.5V Direct Input!)
     5 PSI Fuel Transducer Red Wire -----------------> +5.0V Power Bus Rail
     5 PSI Fuel Transducer Black Wire ---------------> Common Ground Bus Rail (_|_)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PC817 OPTOCOUPLER MODULE & ALTERNATOR TERMINAL 'W' SCHEMATIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Input Side (Alternator Terminal W AC Pulse):
   Alternator Terminal 'W' ---/\/\/\---(1k Ω)----+----> PC817 Channel 1 [IN1+]
                                                |
                                            ---|\|<|--- (1N4007 Diode: Cathode to IN1+, Anode to IN1-)
                                                |
                                                +----> PC817 Channel 1 [IN1-]
                                                |
                                               _|_ (Common GND Bus)

Output Side (Ground-Switching Phototransistor - NO VCC POWER WIRE NEEDED!):
   ELEGOO Terminal [D18] ----------------------------> PC817 Channel 1 Output [O1]
   Common GND Bus Rail (_|_) ------------------------> PC817 Channel 1 Ground [GND]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. RELAY MODULES & VICTRON CHARGER SCHEMATICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Songle Relay 1 (Kohler Engine Crank / Remote Start Circuit):
   [IN1] <-------------------------------------------- ELEGOO Terminal [D26] (Active-LOW: LOW = ON)
   [VCC] <-------------------------------------------- +5.0V Power Bus Rail
   [GND] <-------------------------------------------- Common Ground Bus Rail (_|_)
   [COM] & [NO] Contacts ----------------------------> Wired across Kohler Panel Terminals [3] & [4]

B. Songle Relay 2 (120V Coolant Block Heater / Circulation Pump Control):
   [IN2] <-------------------------------------------- ELEGOO Terminal [D27] (Active-LOW: LOW = ON)
   [VCC] <-------------------------------------------- +5.0V Power Bus Rail
   [GND] <-------------------------------------------- Common Ground Bus Rail (_|_)
   [COM] <-------------------------------------------- +5.0V Power Bus Rail
   [NO]  --------------------------------------------> [BLUE Wire] Outputs +5.0V DC ➔ SSR-40DA [Pin 3 DC+]

C. Victron Orion 48V-to-12V Smart Charger (Solid-State Direct 1-Wire Control):
   Victron Remote Pin [H] <--------------------------- ELEGOO Terminal [D4] (Direct 3.3V Control)
   Victron Remote Pin [L] <--------------------------- Common Ground Bus Rail (_|_)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. EXTERNAL 3-WIRE DIGITAL TEMPERATURE SENSOR SCHEMATIC (DS18B20)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   [RED Wire] (3.3V VCC)   ---------------------------+---> ELEGOO Terminal [3V3]
                                                      |
                                                   /\/\/\ (4.7k Ω Pull-Up Resistor)
                                                      |
   [YELLOW Wire] (1-Wire Data) -----------------------+---> ELEGOO Terminal [D25]
   [BLACK Wire] (Ground GND)   ---------------------------> Common Ground Bus Rail (_|_)

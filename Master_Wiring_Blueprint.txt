================================================================================
     KOHLER 60kW GENSET + ELEGOO ESP-32 BREAKOUT ADAPTER MASTER WIRING BLUEPRINT
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WIRE COLOR CODE CONVENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[RED]          = +5V DC Regulated Power (or +3.3V DC Sensor VCC)
[BLACK]        = Common Ground (GND - All grounds tied together)
[DARK RED]     = +12V DC Battery Power (Fused 5A)
[ORANGE]       = +48V DC Solar Battery Positive Line
[YELLOW]       = Analog & Digital Sensor Signals (Oil, Temp, Fuel, Battery Sense)
[BLUE]         = Relay & Optocoupler Digital Control Signals
[GREEN]        = I2C Data Line (SDA)
[WHITE]        = I2C Clock Line (SCL)
[RJ45]         = Hardwired Ethernet Cable to Router/Switch

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. POWER SUPPLY & DISTRIBUTION BLOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. DROK LM2596 Buck Converter (Set output to EXACTLY 5.0V DC):
   - IN+  <-- [DARK RED] 12V Battery (+) via [5A Inline Fuse] from Generator Starter Battery
   - IN-  <-- [BLACK] 12V Battery (-) Ground from Generator Starter Battery
   - OUT+ --> [RED] 5.0V Output to [Soldered 5V Power Bus Rail]
   - OUT- --> [BLACK] Ground Output to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]

B. Soldered Power Bus Rail Connections:
   - Soldered 5V Bus Rail (Red): Supplies 5V Power to ELEGOO ESP32 VIN, ADS1115, Relays.
   - Soldered GND Bus Rail (Black): Connects ALL Grounds together (ESP32, Sensors, Inverter, Kohler Panel).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ELEGOO ESP-32 USB-C BREAKOUT ADAPTER BOARD PHYSICAL PINOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Power & Ground Connections:
   - Terminal [VIN]    (Left Row 1st)  <-- [RED] Wire from [Soldered 5V Power Bus Rail]
   - Terminal [GND]    (Left / Right)  <-- [BLACK] Wire from [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
   - Terminal [3V3]    (Right Row 1st) --> [RED] Wire to DS18B20 Temp Sensor VCC (Red Wire)

I2C Bus (ADS1115 ADC Module):
   - Terminal [D21]    (Right Row 11th down) --> [GREEN] Wire to ADS1115 ADC Pin [SDA]
   - Terminal [D22]    (Right Row 14th down) --> [WHITE] Wire to ADS1115 ADC Pin [SCL]

Optocoupler Inputs (PC817 Board Outputs - Pure Ground Switching, NO VCC Wire!):
   - PC817 Output [O1] (Ch1): [BLUE] Wire to ELEGOO Terminal [D18] (Alternator Terminal 'W' RPM)
   - PC817 Output [O2] (Ch2): [BLUE] Wire to ELEGOO Terminal [D19] (EG4 18kPV Auto-Start Signal)
   - PC817 Ground [GND]:      [BLACK] Wire to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
   - (NO VCC POWER WIRE AT ALL ON THE PC817 OUTPUT SIDE!)

Relay & Charger Control Outputs:
   - Terminal [D26]    (Left Row 7th down)   --> [BLUE] Wire to Songle Relay 1 [IN1] (Start Command -> Terminals 3 & 4)
   - Terminal [D27]    (Left Row 6th down)   --> [BLUE] Wire to Songle Relay 2 [IN2] (120V Coolant Heater Control)
   - Terminal [D4]     (Right Row 5th down)  --> [BLUE] Wire to Victron Orion Remote Pin [H] (Direct 3.3V Control)

Sensors & Ethernet Module:
   - Terminal [D25]    (Left Row 8th down)   --> [YELLOW] Wire to DS18B20 3-Wire Temp Data (+ 4.7k Ω resistor to 3.3V)
   - Terminal [D34]    (Left Row 12th down)  <-- [YELLOW] Wire from 5 PSI 0.5V-4.5V Fuel Transducer (via 10k/22k divider)
   - Terminal [D14]    (Left Row 5th down)   --> W5500 SPI Ethernet Module [SCLK] (Clock Line)
   - Terminal [D23]    (Right Row 15th down) --> W5500 SPI Ethernet Module [MOSI] (Data Out Line)
   - Terminal [D5]     (Right Row 8th down)  --> W5500 SPI Ethernet Module [SCS / CS] (Chip Select Line)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ADS1115 16-BIT PRECISION ADC MODULE & SENSOR DIVIDERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Power & Data Connections:
   - Pin [VDD]      <-- [RED] Wire from [Soldered 5V Power Bus Rail]
   - Pin [GND]      <-- [BLACK] Wire from [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
   - Pin [SDA]      <-- [GREEN] Wire from ELEGOO ESP32 Terminal [D21]
   - Pin [SCL]      <-- [WHITE] Wire from ELEGOO ESP32 Terminal [D22]

Analog Sensor Channel Inputs & Resistors:
   - Pin [A0] (Oil Pressure):
     <-- [YELLOW] Wire tapped from Kohler Panel Oil Pressure Gauge Terminal [S] via [10k Ohm Resistor]
     * [22k Ohm Resistor] from Pin A0 to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
     * [0.1uF / 104 Ceramic Capacitor] between A0 and GND for noise filtering.

   - Pin [A1] (Water Temp):
     <-- [YELLOW] Wire tapped from Kohler Panel Water Temp Gauge Terminal [S] via [10k Ohm Resistor]
     * [22k Ohm Resistor] from Pin A1 to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
     * [0.1uF / 104 Ceramic Capacitor] between A1 and GND for noise filtering.

   - Pin [A2] (12V Starter Battery Voltage Sense):
     <-- [YELLOW] Wire from 12V Battery (+) via Voltage Divider:
     * 10k Ohm Resistor from 12V(+) line to Pin A2
     * 2.2k Ohm Resistor from Pin A2 to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
     * Calibrated Multiplier: 7.450 (Reads 13.70V accurately)

   - Pin [A3] (48V Solar Battery Bank Voltage Sense):
     <-- [YELLOW] Wire from 48V Solar Battery Bank (+) via Voltage Divider:
     * 47k Ohm Resistor from 48V(+) line to Pin A3
     * 2.7k Ohm Resistor from Pin A3 to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
     * Calibrated Multiplier: 18.407 (49.7 / 2.7)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. KOHLER PANEL 3-TERMINAL ANALOG GAUGES & ALTERNATOR PINOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gauge Signal Tapping (Terminal S):
   - Oil Pressure Gauge: Tap 1 [YELLOW] wire onto Terminal [S] --> Runs to ADS1115 [A0]
   - Water Temp Gauge:   Tap 1 [YELLOW] wire onto Terminal [S] --> Runs to ADS1115 [A1]
   - Alternator Terminal W: Tap 1 [BLUE] wire onto Alternator Terminal [W] --> Runs to PC817 [Ch 1 IN+]
     (With 1N4007 Diode across Ch 1 IN+ and IN-)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. RELAYS & VICTRON 48V-TO-12V CHARGER CONNECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Songle Relay 1 (Kohler Crank / Remote Start Circuit):
   - Terminal [IN1] <-- [BLUE] Wire from ELEGOO ESP32 Terminal [D26]
   - Contacts [COM] & [NO] --> Connect across Kohler Panel Terminals [3] & [4]

Songle Relay 2 (120V Coolant Block Heater Control):
   - Terminal [IN2] <-- [BLUE] Wire from ELEGOO ESP32 Terminal [D27]
   - Terminal [COM] <-- [RED] Wire from [Soldered 5V Power Bus Rail]
   - Terminal [NO]  --> [BLUE] Wire outputs +5.0V DC to Circulation Pump Relay / SSR-40DA [Pin 3 DC+]

Victron Orion 48V-to-12V Smart Charger (Direct Control):
   - Remote Pin [H] <-- [BLUE] Wire from ELEGOO ESP32 Terminal [D4]
   - Remote Pin [L] <-- [BLACK] Wire to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]

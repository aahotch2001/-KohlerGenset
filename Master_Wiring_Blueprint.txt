================================================================================
        KOHLER 60kW GENSET + WT32-ETH01 BUILT-IN LAN MASTER WIRING BLUEPRINT
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WIRE COLOR CODE CONVENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[RED]          = +5V DC Regulated Power
[BLACK]        = Common Ground (GND - All grounds tied together)
[DARK RED]     = +12V DC Battery Power (Fused)
[ORANGE]       = +48V DC Solar Battery Positive Line
[YELLOW]       = Analog Sensor Signals (Oil, Temp, Fuel, Battery Sense)
[BLUE]         = Relay & Optocoupler Digital Control Signals
[GREEN]        = I2C Data Line (SDA)
[WHITE]        = I2C Clock Line (SCL)
[RJ45]         = Hardwired Ethernet Cable to Router/Switch

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. POWER SUPPLY & DISTRIBUTION BLOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. DROK LM2596 Buck Converter (Set output to EXACTLY 5.0V DC):
   - IN+  <-- [DARK RED] 12V Battery (+) via [5A Inline Fuse] from Generator Battery
   - IN-  <-- [BLACK] 12V Battery (-) Ground from Generator Battery
   - OUT+ --> [RED] 5.0V Output to [Soldered 5V Power Bus Rail]
   - OUT- --> [BLACK] Ground Output to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]

B. Soldered Power Bus Rail Connections:
   - Soldered 5V Bus Rail (Red): Supplies 5V Power to WT32-ETH01, ADS1115, Relays, Fuel Transducer.
   - Soldered GND Bus Rail (Black): Connects ALL Grounds together (WT32-ETH01, Sensors, Inverter, Kohler Panel).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WT32-ETH01 v1.4 BOARD PHYSICAL PINOUT CONNECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Power Connections:
   - Terminal [5V]     (Right Row, 2nd from bottom) <-- [RED] Wire from [Soldered 5V Power Bus Rail]
   - Terminal [GND]    (Right Row / Left Row)       <-- [BLACK] Wire from [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]

Ethernet Port:
   - Onboard RJ45 Jack --> Plug standard Cat5e/Cat6 cable directly into router/switch (Mosquitto Broker: 192.168.68.91 / Tailscale: 100.108.173.127)

I2C Bus (ADS1115 ADC):
   - Terminal [IO14]   (GPIO 14 - Left Row 8th down) --> [GREEN] Wire to ADS1115 [SDA]
   - Terminal [IO15]   (GPIO 15 - Left Row 7th down) --> [WHITE] Wire to ADS1115 [SCL]

Optocoupler Inputs (PC817 Board Outputs):
   - Terminal [RXD]    (GPIO 5  - Right Row 7th down)  <-- [BLUE] Wire from PC817 Pin [O1] (Alternator Terminal 'W' AC Pulse Engine RPM & Running Sense)
   - Terminal [IO36]   (GPIO 36 - Left Row 6th down)   <-- [BLUE] Wire from PC817 Pin [O2] (EG4 18kPV Auto-Start Signal)

Relay Control Outputs (Songle Relay & SSR-40DA):
   - Terminal [IO4]    (GPIO 4  - Left Row 11th down) --> [BLUE] Wire to Songle Relay 1 [IN1] (Remote Start Command -> Kohler Terminals 3 & 4)
   - Terminal [IO2]    (GPIO 2  - Left Row 12th down) --> [BLUE] Wire to SSR-40DA [Pin 3 DC+] (120V Coolant Heater Control)

Flashing / Boot Mode Jumper:
   - Terminal [IO0]    (GPIO 0  - Left Row 3rd down)  --> Jumper wire to [GND] when uploading firmware in Arduino IDE

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ADS1115 16-BIT PRECISION ADC MODULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Power Connections:
   - Pin [VDD]      <-- [RED] Wire from [Soldered 5V Power Bus Rail]
   - Pin [GND]      <-- [BLACK] Wire from [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
   - Pin [SDA]      <-- [GREEN] Wire from WT32-ETH01 Pin [IO14]
   - Pin [SCL]      <-- [WHITE] Wire from WT32-ETH01 Pin [IO15]

Analog Sensor Channel Inputs:
   - Pin [A0] (Oil Pressure):
     <-- [YELLOW] Wire tapped from Kohler Panel Oil Pressure Gauge Terminal [S] via [10k Ohm Resistor]
     * [22k Ohm Resistor] from Pin A0 to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
     * [0.1uF / 104 Ceramic Capacitor] between A0 and GND for noise filtering.

   - Pin [A1] (Water Temp):
     <-- [YELLOW] Wire tapped from Kohler Panel Water Temp Gauge Terminal [S] via [10k Ohm Resistor]
     * [22k Ohm Resistor] from Pin A1 to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
     * [0.1uF / 104 Ceramic Capacitor] between A1 and GND for noise filtering.

   - Pin [A2] (12V Starter Battery Voltage Sense):
     <-- [YELLOW] Wire from 12V Battery (+) via Resistor Voltage Divider:
     * 10k Ohm Resistor from 12V(+) line to Pin A2
     * 2.2k Ohm Resistor from Pin A2 to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]

   - Pin [A3] (48V Solar Battery Bank Voltage Sense):
     <-- [YELLOW] Wire from 48V Solar Battery Bank (+) 14-2 Romex wire via Resistor Divider:
     * 47k Ohm Resistor from 48V(+) line to Pin A3
     * 2.7k Ohm Resistor from Pin A3 to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. KOHLER PANEL 3-TERMINAL ANALOG GAUGES PINOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each analog gauge on the Kohler Decision Maker 3 panel has 3 rear terminals:
   - Terminal [I] (or "+"): 12V Switched Power from Panel (DO NOT TOUCH)
   - Terminal [G] (or "-"): Panel Ground (DO NOT TOUCH)
   - Terminal [S] (SENDER): THIS IS THE SIGNAL TERMINAL YOU TAP INTO!

Tapping Instructions:
   - Oil Pressure Gauge: Tap 1 [YELLOW] wire onto Terminal [S] --> Runs to ADS1115 [A0]
   - Water Temp Gauge:   Tap 1 [YELLOW] wire onto Terminal [S] --> Runs to ADS1115 [A1]
   - Alternator Terminal W (RPM & Frequency): Tap 1 [BLUE] wire onto Alternator Terminal [W] --> Runs to PC817 [Ch 3 IN+] via [1k Ohm Resistor]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PC817 OPTOCOUPLER MODULE & ALTERNATOR TERMINAL 'W' RESISTOR WIRING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Alternator Terminal 'W' (AC Voltage Pulse) Input Protection:
   - Alternator Terminal [W] outputs 12V-20V AC pulses when engine is running.
   - Install a [1k Ohm 1/4W Resistor] in series on the signal wire from Terminal [W] to PC817 [Ch 1 IN+].
   - Install a [1N4007 or 1N4148 Diode] across PC817 [Ch 1 IN+] and [Ch 1 IN-]
     (Cathode line mark to IN+, Anode to IN-) to protect optocoupler LED against reverse AC half-cycles.
   - PC817 [Ch 1 IN-] connects to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail].

PC817 Board Output Connections to WT32-ETH01:
   - PC817 [VCC] --> [RED] Wire to WT32-ETH01 [3V3] Pin
   - PC817 [GND] --> [BLACK] Wire to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
   - PC817 [O1]  --> [BLUE] Wire to WT32-ETH01 Pin [IO35] (Left Row 10th down - Alternator Terminal 'W' RPM)
   - PC817 [O2]  --> [BLUE] Wire to WT32-ETH01 Pin [IO36] (Left Row 6th down - EG4 Inverter Auto-Start)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. EXTERNAL 3-WIRE DIGITAL TEMPERATURE SENSOR SCHEMATIC (DS18B20 / OneWire)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Power & Data Connections:
   - [RED Wire]    (VCC)  <-- Connect to ESP32 [3V3] Pin (or 5V Power Bus Rail)
   - [BLACK Wire]  (GND)  <-- Connect to [Soldered Common Ground Bus Rail]
   - [YELLOW Wire] (Data) --> Connect to ESP32 Pin [D25] (GPIO 25)

Pull-Up Resistor Requirement:
   - Install a [4.7k Ohm 1/4W Resistor] connected between the [RED Wire (3.3V)]
     and the [YELLOW Wire (Data)] right at the sensor termination point.

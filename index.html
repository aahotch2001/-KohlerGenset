🛡️ Safety & Operating Rules

  • START: Relays engage ➔ Status shows CRANKING ➔ Terminal W confirms RPM > 400 ➔ Status turns to RUNNING.
  • STOP: Relay disengages immediately ➔ Engine runs down Kohler internal cooldown (COOLDOWN) ➔ RPM == 0 ➔ Status turns to STOPPED.
  • 15s Over-crank Safety: If starter cranks >15 seconds without engine firing, relay automatically disengages.
  • Smart Victron 12V Charger: Turns ON when 12V Starter Battery drops below 12.3V, and turns OFF when charged to 13.8V (0mA 48V standby drain).
